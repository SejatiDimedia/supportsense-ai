# Surface Brief: SupportSense AI React Console

## Scope and Mode

Primary route: `frontend/src/App.tsx`
Mode: `Operate`

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-density operational console for enterprise AI customer support where intelligence is transparent, refusing the generic floating-cards and opaque conversational bubble pattern.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep graphite background (#0A0E17, #111827) with hairline 1px borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)), crisp monospaced tabular numerals for metrics and latency, and dedicated semantic tints for route classification: emerald for RAG, violet for KAG, amber for Hybrid, blue for Direct, and crimson for Escalated. No emojis or emoticons anywhere.

### STORY
The operator monitors real-time telemetry (PostgreSQL, ChromaDB, and escalation queues), issues test queries or clicks scenario chips, immediately sees exact routing classification with latency, confidence score, and opens the grounding drawer to audit verified database facts.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A fixed top bar with brand mark, session ID, and live telemetry chip bar; a main dual-pane workspace with chat stream on the left (two-thirds) and active grounding/telemetry drawer on the right (one-third); and an ergonomic bottom input console with keyboard shortcuts.

### FORM
Dark-first developer and operator console with syntax-derived state accents and hairline seams; candidate 3 of grounded list, seed key 77a13c9d.
